### Cost Structure Assumptions

#### COGS (% of Revenue)
- **OpenAI API Costs:** $0.50–2.00 per active user per month
  - Embeddings: ~$0.10–0.30/user/month
  - Chat completions: ~$0.40–1.70/user/month
  - Scales with feature adoption and model improvements
- **Supabase (Database):** ~$25–500/month scaling with usage
  - Starts at free tier ($0), scales to ~$500/month by end of Year 2
- **Infrastructure (Redis, hosting, monitoring):** ~$100–500/month
  - Self-hosted Redis cluster, Docker-based deployments
- **Total COGS at maturity:** 15–20% of revenue
